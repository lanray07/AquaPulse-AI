# AquaPulse AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 23, 2026

AquaPulse AI is designed as a local-first hydration tracker. The app stores your hydration profile, reminder preferences, drink entries, streaks, achievements, and subscription state on your device using Apple's local storage technologies.

## Data We Do Not Collect

We do not operate a backend service for AquaPulse AI and do not collect, sell, or share your personal hydration data.

## Data Stored On Your Device

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

- Name or nickname entered during onboarding
- Age range, weight, activity level, preferred cup size, and hydration goal
- Drink entries, drink types, reminder settings, streaks, achievements, and history
- Subscription status provided by Apple's StoreKit APIs

## Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. If you choose to connect Apple Health, AquaPulse AI may request permission to read or write water intake data. You can manage Apple Health permissions at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

## Notifications

AquaPulse AI uses local notifications for hydration reminders. Notification preferences are managed on your device.

## Apple Watch and Widgets

Apple Watch and widget features may use Apple platform services to show hydration progress and quick logging controls. This information is intended to remain within your devices and Apple account ecosystem.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
